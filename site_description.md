## Operational notes

- A granted badge unlocks the door; the door position sensor logs
  when the door physically opens and closes.
- Doors with *entry only* readers have push-bar exits: leaving
  through them opens the door but records no badge event.
- The Server Room (D17) is restricted to a small number of
  cardholders and is the most security-sensitive area on site.
- Deliveries arrive most Tuesday and Thursday mornings around
  10:00; the Loading Dock can stand open while trucks unload.
- A fire evacuation drill was held mid-morning on Tuesday 18 August 2026.
- The cleaning crew works evenings including weekends, and security
  guards patrol the building day and night.
- Device clocks are set manually and are not centrally synchronised.

Data covers Monday 10 August 2026 to Sunday 23 August 2026 inclusive.
